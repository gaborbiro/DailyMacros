## Long description (max 4000 chars)

```
Daily Macros turns a photo into a full nutrition log — no manual food search, no barcode scanning, no guesswork.

📸 SNAP A PHOTO, GET INSTANT MACROS
Take a picture of your meal (or type a quick description) and Daily Macros recognises the food and estimates calories, protein, fat, saturated fat, carbs, sugar, salt and fibre for you.

🎯 SEE YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
Every entry rolls up into a daily summary with progress bars against your own targets, so you always know how much room you have left for the day.

📈 WATCH YOUR TRENDS OVER TIME
Switch between days, weeks and months to see how your calories and macros move over time — useful for spotting patterns, not just logging one meal at a time.

⭐ ONE-TAP HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
Pin your favourite and most frequent meals as home screen widgets, or mark them as Quick Picks, so logging a repeat meal takes one tap instead of a full re-scan.

✏️ EDIT ANYTHING
Recognition is a starting point, not the final word — adjust names, descriptions, nutrition values and even the recorded time for any entry.

🔍 FOUND A DIFFERENT VARIANT?
Add extra photos (like the nutrition label) to any entry to help the AI narrow down the exact product variant.

☁️ YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
Your food diary lives on your device. Optional backup goes to your own Google Drive — never to the developer's servers.

🔒 PRIVACY BY DESIGN
No account or sign-up required. Meal photos and descriptions are sent only to power the nutrition analysis and are not stored by the developer. See the in-app Privacy & Data section for full details.

Whether you're counting calories, hitting a protein target, watching sugar and salt, or just curious what's actually in your food, Daily Macros keeps your nutrition diary a photo away.
```
(~1650 chars — well under the 4000 limit; can expand if we want more keyword coverage)
