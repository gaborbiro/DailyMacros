How it works

  1. Show it what you ate — Snap a photo. Add a few words if they help; typing is optional.
  2. Check what it inferred — Daily Macros identifies the foods and portions and estimates the nutrition. You can see the assumptions it made.
  3. Fix anything that’s off — Add a clarification or another photo and re-run the analysis.
  4. It’s in your diary — Your daily totals update automatically.

Favourite meals get a one-tap home-screen widget, so most days you never need to open the app at all.
